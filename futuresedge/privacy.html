FuturesEdge Mini privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Desktop data

Your calculator settings, checklists and journal are stored in your Windows user profile by default. Journal records may include trading details or notes you enter. You can export or back up this data. Keep journal content and backups private.

Optional cloud synchronization uses the project you configure and sends the journal data needed for that feature. The app does not request brokerage credentials. Local diagnostics record timestamps, component names and error types. Local sponsor counters record placements shown and clicked by campaign ID. The app does not automatically transmit either log.

Website and external links

Loading the product page, an installer, or an optional update or resources check contacts the site host. Its hosting infrastructure may process standard request information. Optional download counts are off by default and stored only in your browser. You can reset them on the download page. Download links can carry public campaign tags.

When you choose to open a sponsor or affiliate link, the destination’s privacy policy applies. Sending an inquiry shares the information in your email with CapitalGuildX so we can respond. Please contact capitalguildx@gmail.com with privacy, access or deletion requests.
