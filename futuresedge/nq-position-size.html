NQ position size calculator

Enter a dollar risk budget and a stop distance in points. The calculator rounds down to whole contracts so the estimated stop risk stays within that budget.

Estimates exclude commissions, fees, slippage and margin requirements.

Worked example

A 25-point stop costs $500 per contract. With a $1200 risk budget, the calculator returns 2 contracts and $1000.00 in estimated stop risk.

Risk per contract = stop points × value per point. Contracts = risk budget ÷ risk per contract, rounded down. Confirm contract specifications with your exchange or broker. CME Group contract reference.
